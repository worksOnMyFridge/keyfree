# Fifth review: the whole lifecycle (code version v18, 2026-11-06)

A separate AI reviewer session, which had no part in writing the code, was asked to attack Keyfree before (install,
setup, configuration, supply chain), during (an agent or CI using it, the council governing it) and after (deployed
contracts, upgrades, retirement, monitoring) it runs, focusing on what the earlier reviews had not covered. Read-only
toward the chains and the repository; its probe tests ran in a copy. Ids are Z1..Z12 here (the reviewer wrote R1..R12;
renamed so they do not clash with the requirement ids in AUDIT.md). What was done about each: [AUDIT.md](../AUDIT.md).

## Verdict (the reviewer's words, shortened)

The on-chain policy held: nothing gets a signature for code that is not allowed, and the v18 callback change is
correct. The serious risks were in the human layer around it: the setup path put all three council keys next to the
agent, and the shipped alarm could not fire in time for the delays it guards.

## Checked and found safe

- v18 (`park`, `handle_signed`, `on_confirmed`): a re-sign never overwrites a confirmed line and opens nothing; the
  proved hash is kept.
- The explorer page: every on-chain string is escaped, links must be https, the CSP allows no outside scripts.
- Storage growth by strangers: `confirm` writes nothing, proposals are capped (8 rules, 3 role changes), the agent's
  growth is bounded by the daily budget.
- The factory and CREATE2: only the policy may call; the code hash binds the address; no metamorphic redeploy after
  Cancun; used salts are refused.
- TreasuryProxy: no upgrade-and-call, no admin change, no initializer; V2's storage does not collide with ERC-1967 slots.
- Supply chain: `npm ci --ignore-scripts`, actions pinned to commits, lockfile entries from registry.npmjs.org with
  integrity hashes; cargo-near and gitleaks downloads checksum-verified; the Docker image pinned by digest.
- The CI script: inputs reach it through the environment, the key file is mktemp 0600 and removed.
- Error output: malformed keys are not echoed; RPC URLs are reduced to the host.
- The agent-key check refuses full-access keys and keys with extra methods (assuming an honest node).

## Not covered by the reviewer

The nearcore sandbox, a reproducible rebuild of the wasm, the explorer in a real browser, the Read MPC payload
encoding beyond reading the code, the attack bench and `check_claims`, the npm package contents beyond the lockfile.
